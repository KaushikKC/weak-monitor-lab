# Clustered confidence intervals on existing results (roadmap A1)

Date: 2026-10-21. Code: commit `c41c9b3` (`src/weak_monitor_lab/intervals.py`, `wml intervals`). Input: `data/export/episodes.csv` and `judgements.csv` as of commit `b387927`. No model calls; no run output changed. Long-format CSV of every cell: [2026-10-21-clustered-intervals.csv](2026-10-21-clustered-intervals.csv).

## Method

* **Episodes, classes, detectors.** The same as `wml summarize`. Only submitted episodes are used. Per (episode, monitor, condition), the score is the mean over valid outputs, and episodes with no valid output are left out and counted in "no valid output".
  * Positives: `exploit_attempt`.
  * Negative classes come from `category_v2` and the gap G. Honest failures are `ordinary_failure`, split by G > 0 (gap-matched), G = 0 and G < 0. Honest successes are `honest_success`.
  * Detectors, at a fixed cut of 0.5:
    * `score≥0.5`;
    * `checklist`, the v3/v5 checklist rule, where a missing checklist counts as not flagged and AUC is computed on the 0/1 flag;
    * `rule-v2 OR <LLM>`, scored as the max of the two. An invalid LLM output gives no combined output.
* **Point estimates match summarize.** Every row of the "Pre-registered criteria" and "Scripted fixtures by policy group" sections (77 rows), and every AUC in "Monitor results" (180 cells), equals `data/export/summarize-check.txt`.
* **Intervals.**
  * `W`: Wilson 95%, for proportions only, as before.
  * `C`: 95% percentile bootstrap. It draws 10,000 resamples per dataset with seed 20261021, each drawing the dataset's base scenarios with replacement; all episodes of a drawn scenario come along. One set of draws per dataset is shared by every metric of that dataset.
  * "(m undef.)": the number of resamples in which the metric is undefined because its class or a side of the AUC is absent. These resamples are dropped for that metric.
  * When k = 0 or k = n, every resample gives the same proportion, so the `C` interval has zero width by construction.
* **AUC columns.**
  * "exploit_attempt vs gap-matched (G>0)" is the pre-registered R1 AUC.
  * "… vs rest" are the AUCs of the summarize "Monitor results" table, over all submitted episodes and over the dev and test splits. For `checklist` rows these AUCs are new: summarize reports them for scores only.
* **Fixture columns (D3 only).** The policy groups are those of the summarize fixture table.
* **Not included:** dev-fit threshold rows; the calibration runs, which have no monitor judgements; the 2026-10-08 combined-detector study, whose data does not exist yet.
